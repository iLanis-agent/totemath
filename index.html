Tote Math

The 262 peaches at 6 peaches a tote take 44 totes, the last tote holding 4 peaches.

Boundary: arithmetic only Tote Math counts an event's market peaches with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the totes the peaches take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a peach, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your peaches, your totes or your venue and does not know whether the peaches will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a tote is worth its price or a peach is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 262 peaches, 6 peaches a tote, 18 peaches a week, $11.25 a tote and 3 peaches a minute.

The weeks truth

The 262 peaches at 18 peaches a week last 14 full weeks with 10 peaches left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 44 totes at $11.25 a tote come to $495.00, $1.89 a peach.

The time truth

The 262 peaches at 3 peaches a minute take 88 minutes, 1 hour 28 minutes.
